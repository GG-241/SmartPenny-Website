Questions

The ones people ask

Pricing

What does the app cost?

The app is currently free to use during its testing period. All features are available at no cost—there are no accounts, advertisements, subscriptions, or trial limits. If optional paid features are introduced in the future, they will be additional offerings rather than restrictions on the functionality you already use.

Account and sign-in

Do I need to create an account?

No. You can open the app and start using it immediately. Signing in with Apple or Google is only required if you choose to enable cloud sync or share data with other people.

Data and privacy

Where is my data stored?

Your data is stored locally on your phone. Nothing is uploaded unless you explicitly enable sync or backup. Receipts are processed on your device, so receipt images do not leave your phone. You can export or permanently delete your data at any time from Settings.

Importing and exporting

Can I import my existing transaction history?

Yes. You can import data from YNAB, Splitwise, Revolut, N26, or Monzo, as well as from any CSV or Excel file. Duplicate entries are detected during import. You can also export your history to CSV, Excel, PDF, or a complete JSON backup at any time.

Backup and recovery

What happens if I lose my phone?

You can protect your data by enabling encrypted sync or cloud backup, or by saving an exported JSON backup in a secure location. To restore your data on a new phone, go to Settings → Export / Import and select your backup file.

Offline use

Does the app work without an internet connection?

Yes. The app is designed to work fully offline. All core features—including expense tracking, bill splitting, and settling up with a QR code—work without an internet connection. Internet access is only needed for optional sync and backup features.
